Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen with a delivery app

Hearth & Harvest started as a twelve-seat kitchen on Willow Street, where people queued for one thing: honest, slow-cooked food made the same day. When the queue got too long, we moved the kitchen online.

Today our chefs cook for your table the way they would cook for theirs — real ingredients, real recipes, no shortcuts. You just tell us what you fancy.

  • Recipes developed by our head chef, not a manufacturer
  • Local suppliers for 80% of everything we buy
  • Every order labelled with full allergen information
